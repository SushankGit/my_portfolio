About Sushank Shrestha

MIS Officer at Yeti Brewery Limited.

I oversee the design and scaling of information systems that directly support industrial and manufacturing operations. At Yeti Brewery, I lead the technical development of platforms such as Sales MIS, Secondary Sales Analytics, and Inventory Reconciliation Systems. These solutions are embedded in daily workflows—empowering sales representatives, plant managers, auditors, and executives to manage millions of rupees in transactions and hundreds of thousands of production crates with accuracy and efficiency.

At Yeti Brewery, my role is defined by a commitment to optimization, zero data loss, and seamless user experiences. By combining robust relational database design with automated ETL pipelines, I’ve eliminated thousands of hours of manual reporting, ensuring that decision-makers always have timely, reliable insights.

I approach system engineering not just as a technical exercise but as a discipline that bridges business strategy and IT execution. My academic and professional journey has taught me to view MIS as the nervous system of an enterprise—where every dataset, report, and dashboard contributes to operational clarity and strategic foresight.
